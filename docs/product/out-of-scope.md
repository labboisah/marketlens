# Out of Scope for MVP

The following items are explicitly later phases. They should not be allowed to
expand the MVP unless product leadership intentionally re-scopes the release.

## Later Phases

- Full accounting.
- AI forecasting.
- Automatic bank-transfer verification.
- E-commerce.
- Restaurant management.
- Hotel management.
- Fuel-station module.
- Pharmacy prescription processing.
- Payroll.
- Advanced CRM.
- Field-sales application.
- iOS release.
- Multi-region cloud deployment.
- Kubernetes.

## Scope Control Rules

- Do not build industry-specific modules before the general retail MVP is
  stable.
- Do not add advanced automation before core sales, stock, payments, debt,
  synchronization, and monitoring flows are reliable.
- Do not introduce infrastructure complexity such as Kubernetes or multi-region
  deployment before commercial demand requires it.
- Do not implement full accounting until the MVP ledger, expense, debt, and
  report foundations are validated with pilot users.
