## Shareholders Monitoring

Endpoints:

- `/api/v1/monitoring/shareholders`
- `/api/v1/monitoring/shareholders/{uuid}`
- `/api/v1/monitoring/shareholders/ownership`
- `/api/v1/monitoring/profit-periods`
- `/api/v1/monitoring/profit-distributions`

Notes:

- Platform monitoring is read-only. All ownership changes must be recorded via transactions and originate from authorized business workflows.
